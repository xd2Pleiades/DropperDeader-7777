The Federation consists of 8 planets, the capital is Karo, the government resides in the Octavosol National Assembly,
currently as Emergency Assembly Union. A Tricameral Government ruled the Federation, first the 8 Planetary Councillors
and two Consuls, this body is known as Deficis. The Senate of 828 senators, and the States of Representative of 126,981
set members unless they were added by law.

The SDP of the Federation is 868.98 t and is the top seventy-eight military in the galaxy, considering the Arcturus
Assembly Forces has experience and technologies from the Great War; the Sons of Mars contributing much to the military
might of Octavosol.

The capital Karo has a population of fifty-three trillion, and is made up of twelve city district and twenty industrial
district, the mining districts have been continuously built into two useable districts.
